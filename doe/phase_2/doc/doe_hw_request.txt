QUANTUM PLATFORM JUSTIFICATION: ENTANGLED TRIO
Project: U.S. Department of Energy OTC Challenge (Phase 2)
Objective: Executing Benders-QAMOO quantum algorithm for optimal sizing and siting of components for the U.S. microgrid.

1. PRIMARY MODALITY CHOICE
IBM Quantum QPUs are requested, since we implement a Samplomatic error mitigation pipeline (PEC+SLC+PNA) that can mitigate device-specific noise to maximize QPU usage. Offline simulation scales up to 30 qubits but cannot replicate hardware noise.

2. BENCHMARKING DETAILS
* Baseline: 20–30 qubit verification against classical Tensor Network simulation (Qiskit Aer) and validated by Pandapower for AC power-flow feasibility.
* Target: Production QPU runs vs. classical MIP solvers (CPLEX/Gurobi).

3. TASKS AND SHOTS
* QPU Job: 320 circuits (10 Pareto points x 32 twirling randomizations) @ 125 shots/circuit = 40,000 shots.
* Job Volume: 50 to 100 iterative jobs per workflow. Parameter optimization is pre-trained via classical simulation; QPU execution is reserved strictly for quantum Pareto evaluations.

4. QPU ARCHITECTURE JUSTIFICATION
Requested: 156-qubit Heron-class QPUs (e.g., Kingston, Fez). Alternative: 120-qubit Eagle-class.
* Scale: Heron supports up to 156 qubits, enabling direct variable encoding of grid cases up to 156 bus widths.
* Fidelity: Heron's high 2-qubit gate fidelity (>99%) and low cross-talk are required for Samplomatic to resolve the signal (fails on noisier Eagle systems).
* Integration: Native support for Qiskit Executor primitives.

5. EXPECTED HOURS OF USAGE
Requested: 3 hours of active QPU runtime.
* Usage: 200–300 jobs total across several grid cases (eg: IEEE-14, IEEE-33 and their sub-partitions).
* Tier: Bypasses the 10 min/month IBM Open Plan cap and grants access to IBM Heron.
