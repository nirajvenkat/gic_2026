QUANTUM PLATFORM JUSTIFICATION: ENTANGLED TRIO
Project: Mitsubishi Chemical / AIST Challenge (Phase 2)
Objective: Modeling EUV photoabsorption and Auger cascades in 4-iodo-2-methylphenol via a hybrid GQE, q-sc-EOM, and OCA pipeline.

1. PRIMARY MODALITY CHOICE AND WHY
GPU state-vector simulation (PennyLane/CUDA-Q). Target: transition RDMs for core-ionized and doubly-ionized states to plot a KE spectrum for Auger electrons. Active space of 16-18 spatial orbitals (32-36 qubits) required for 92 eV EUV cascade (Iodine 4d + valence), selected via AVAS and pseudopotential methods to fit within our 36-qubit simulation limit. Deterministic simulation is mandatory to achieve chemical accuracy.

2. BENCHMARKING DETAILS
Benchmarked against classical chemistry:
* H2O Auger baseline (7 orbitals / 14 qubits) vs FCI (PySCF).
* IMePh target CAS(24e, 18o) (36 qubits) or CAS(16e, 16o) (32 qubits) vs Selected Configuration Interaction (SCI) and OpenMolcas.

3. TASKS AND SHOTS
Shots: 0 (analytical state-vector expectation values via cudaq.observe()).
Tasks: 300,000 to 500,000 CUDAQ kernel executions for GQE gradient descent and q-sc-EOM matrix construction (2,500 amplitudes/molecule). Subsequent OCA tensor contractions will utilize cuBLAS library acceleration.

4. GPU ARCHITECTURE JUSTIFICATION
Requested: 8xB200-180GB-SXM6 (Alternative: 8xH100-80GB-SXM5).
FP64 state-vector memory: 34 qubits (274.8 GB), 35 qubits (549.7 GB), 36 qubits (1,099.5 GB).
Blackwell (B200-SXM6) Advantages:
* Latency: Halves the GPU count for NVLink distribution vs H100 (2 vs 4 GPUs for 34 qubits); SXM6 NVLink-5 bandwidth (1.8 TB/s) minimizes inter-GPU communication bottleneck.
* Capacity: 8xB200 offers 1,440 GB VRAM, fitting the 36-qubit space (1,099.5 GB) on a single node. 8xH100 is capped at 640 GB and will go out of memory.
* Precision: SXM6 native FP64 throughput prevents rounding errors in the kinetic energy distributions.

5. EXPECTED HOURS OF USAGE
Requested: 125 GPU hours on qBraid.
* H2O calibration baseline: ~10-15 hours.
* Production: Caching molecular integrals and reusing optimization parameters will drastically reduce runtime for subsequent IMePh runs.
* CAS(24e, 18o) and CAS(16e, 16o) active space trims guarantee all production sweeps complete within the 125-hour allocation. Prototyping will be restricted to local CPU/GPU resources.
